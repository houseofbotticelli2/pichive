PicHive

Sorts your photos into albums in Apple Photos, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your photos never leave your devices.

  • New photos, sorted in minutes. Swipe each one into a suggested album, leave it out, or skip it.
  • Your backlog, a group at a time. Trips, days out and events are found for you, ready to add as albums.
  • Planned albums. Set up "Italy 2027" before you go, and photos from the trip are added for you.
  • Always undoable. Every change is listed in History with Undo. The app never deletes a photo.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

In short: we don't collect any data. Your photos are analyzed on your own devices and never leave them. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your photos

PicHive reads your photo library with your permission so it can suggest albums and add photos to them in Apple Photos. All of this happens on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Photos, thumbnails and the app's analysis of them are never uploaded anywhere. The app never deletes photos.

Place names

To give places readable names (for example "Fenway Park"), the app asks Apple Maps about a photo's map coordinates. Only the coordinates are sent, never the photo. Apple handles these requests under Apple's privacy policy.

Location and contacts (optional)

If you choose to set your home from where you are now, or from your own contact card, the app uses that once to find your home. A contact card's address is looked up with Apple Maps to place it on the map. Neither is sent to us.

Sync between your devices

Your choices (for example photos you left out, your named places and your History) sync between your devices through your own private iCloud, using Apple's CloudKit. This data is encrypted so that only your devices can read it. We can't see it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Analytics

The App Store version collects nothing. During the beta test (TestFlight), the app keeps simple counts on the device (for example how many suggestions were accepted). They contain no photos, places or album names, and they're shared only if a tester chooses to send them.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions: support@botticelli.llc.

Support

Does the app delete or move my photos?

Never. It only adds photos to albums. Anything it added can be undone from History.

Why didn't a planned album fill straight away?

iPhone doesn't let apps act the moment a photo is taken. Photos are added the next time you open the app, or in background time when iPhone allows it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Settings, then Subscription, then Manage subscription. Or open your Apple Account settings on any device.

Something's not right

Email support@botticelli.llc with what you saw and which device you're using.
